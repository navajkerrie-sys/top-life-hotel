A neighbourhood bar with a proper kitchen

Top Life Hotel started as a place to share a plate of nyama choma after work, and it still is. The grill stays lit all evening, the sound system is tuned for conversation until 9pm, then turned up.

Bring a group. We seat parties of up to 20 and can set aside a corner for birthdays and send-offs.
